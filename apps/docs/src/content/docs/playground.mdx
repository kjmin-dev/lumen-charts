## Things to try

- **Pan & zoom**: drag horizontally to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, drag any price axis vertically to scale it manually, and double-click the axis to turn autoscaling back on.
- **Independent indicator panes**: toggle **RSI pane** to switch `paneWeight` between an independent oscillator pane and a hidden overlay.
- **GPU mark layers**: toggle **Book + Trades** to reveal the WebGL2 order-book heatmap behind the candles and trade execution scatters on top.
- **Interactive drawings**: choose a tool from the **Draw** dropdown, click or drag on the chart to place points (with `nearest` crosshair), then drag handles to edit or press `Delete` to remove.
- **Save & restore state**: click **Save state** to inspect the exact versioned `ChartState` JSON, pan or change tools, and click **Restore** to jump back atomically.
- **Keyboard navigation**: press `Tab` to focus the chart navigator, then use Arrow keys, `Home`/`End`, `+`/`-`, and `0` to explore bars with screen-reader announcements.
