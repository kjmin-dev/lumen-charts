	</Card>
	<Card title="Built-in & custom indicators" icon="seti:graphql">

		chaining, independent indicator panes, and standalone ES module custom kernels.
	</Card>
	<Card title="GPU heatmaps & scatter series" icon="sun">

		grids, density coloring, mark datafeeds and indicators over marks.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Drawings, primitives & state" icon="pencil">
